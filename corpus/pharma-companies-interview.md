# Top Pharma Companies for DS/AI Interviews

A study guide to the 30 largest pharma companies (by 2025 pharma revenue), with
what each does, flagship products, and where data science / AI fits. Use this
to answer "why us?" and to hold a credible business conversation.

## Top 30 at a glance

### 1. Johnson & Johnson (US, JNJ) — ~$98B
Diversified pharma + medtech. Immunology (Stelara, Tremfya), oncology
(Darzalex, Carvykti), neuroscience (Spravato), plus surgical/orthopedic devices.
**DS/AI**: digital surgery, surgical robotics, real-world evidence across a huge
patient dataset.

### 2. Roche (Switzerland, RO) — ~$80B
Oncology via Genentech (Tecentriq, Avastin, Herceptin), neuroscience (Ocrevus),
ophthalmology (Vabysmo), and **the world's largest diagnostics business**.
**DS/AI**: digital pathology, imaging, Flatiron Health (oncology RWE) — one of
the most data-rich pharma companies.

### 3. Eli Lilly (US, LLY) — ~$80B
Diabetes/obesity (Mounjaro, Zepbound — tirzepatide), Alzheimer's (donanemab),
oncology, immunology. Best-performing large-cap pharma on GLP-1 growth.
**DS/AI**: clinical-trial ML, digital health, heavy AI drug-discovery partnerships.

### 4. Merck & Co. (US, MRK) — ~$67B
Oncology (Keytruda, the biggest-selling drug), vaccines (Gardasil), animal
health, HIV. **DS/AI**: biomarker discovery, trial analytics, target ID.

### 5. AbbVie (US, ABBV) — ~$64B
Immunology (Skyrizi, Rinvoq; formerly Humira), neuroscience (Botox, Vraylar),
oncology. **DS/AI**: R&D analytics, commercial/market-access modeling.

### 6. Pfizer (US, PFE) — ~$64B
Broad portfolio: vaccines (Comirnaty), oncology, internal medicine, rare
disease. **DS/AI**: AI drug discovery collaborations, manufacturing/QC,
large-scale clinical data.

### 7. AstraZeneca (UK, AZN) — ~$61B
Oncology (Tagrisso, Imfinzi, Enhertu w/ Daiichi), respiratory (Symbicort),
cardiovascular/renal (Farxiga), rare disease (Alexion).
**DS/AI**: leader in AI drug discovery and AI-based medical imaging.

### 8. Sanofi (France, SNY) — ~$57B
Immunology (Dupixent), vaccines, rare disease (Sanofi Genzyme).
**DS/AI**: "plai" internal AI platform, digital pipeline.

### 9. Novartis (Switzerland, NVS) — ~$57B
Oncology (Kisqali, Scemblix), immunology (Cosentyx), cardiovascular (Entresto),
gene therapy (Zolgensma). Generics spun off as Sandoz.
**DS/AI**: data science at scale, digital therapeutics, radioligand therapy.

### 10. Bayer (Germany, BAYN) — ~$53B
Pharma (oncology, cardiology like Xarelto, women's health) + crop science +
consumer health. **DS/AI**: drug discovery, radiology AI.

### 11. Novo Nordisk (Denmark, NVO) — ~$51B
Diabetes/obesity (Ozempic, Wegovy — semaglutide), haemophilia, growth
disorders. **DS/AI**: trial analytics, digital adherence tools.

### 12. Bristol Myers Squibb (US, BMY) — ~$49B
Oncology (Opdivo, Revlimid), hematology (Carvykti), cardiovascular (Eliquis).
**DS/AI**: discovery, trial design.

### 13. GSK (UK, GSK) — ~$44B
Vaccines (Shingrix, Arexvy), HIV (ViiV), respiratory, a growing oncology

### 14. Amgen (US, AMGN) — ~$38B

### 30. Biogen (US, BIIB) — ~$10B

## Where DS/AI jobs actually are

- **Discovery**: target ID, protein/molecule generation, ADMET/property
  prediction, protein language models (AlphaFold, ESM).
- **Clinical**: patient stratification/enrichment, trial-site selection,
  recruitment/dropout prediction, real-world evidence.
- **Diagnostics/imaging**: digital pathology, radiology, digital biomarkers.
- **Manufacturing/QC**: yield and deviation prediction, visual inspection,
  digital twins.
- **Commercial/medical**: market access, HCP engagement (compliant),
  pharmacovigilance, medical-literature automation.

## Interview talking points

- Know **1–2 flagship drugs** and a **recent AI initiative** per company.
- Always bring **regulated-data context**: HIPAA/PHI, GDPR, FDA SaMD, GxP,
  audit trails.
- Mention **calibration, subgroup fairness, and external validation** for
  clinical models.
- For discovery roles: graph neural nets, protein language models, generative
  chemistry, and data quality (data > model complexity in small-data settings).
- Show you understand **GxP / 21 CFR Part 11** if the role touches regulated
  manufacturing or submissions.
